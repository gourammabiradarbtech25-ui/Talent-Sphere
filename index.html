WELCOME TO TALENTSPHERE!

Every student has a unique talent. TalentSphere is a platform where students can showcase their abilities, share their creativity, and receive recognition for their skills.

Whether you love singing, dancing, acting, art, coding, storytelling, or other activities, TalentSphere gives you an opportunity to shine.

ABOUT TALENTSPHERE

TalentSphere aims to help students discover and showcase their hidden talents through a common platform.

Our goal is to provide equal opportunities, encourage creativity, build confidence, and recognize individual strengths rather than focusing only on popularity.

HOW TALENTSPHERE WORKS

  1. Scan the TalentSphere QR code.
  2. Open the Google registration form.
  3. Enter your personal and college details.
  4. Select your talent category.
  5. Describe your talent.
  6. Upload your talent performance video through the form.
  7. Submit your registration.
  8. Receive confirmation of your submission.
